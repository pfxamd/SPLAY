Typography already has a voice. A variable font has a whole room full of them. SPLAY is a fictional experimental type studio designed to let you listen, interrupt and respond.

Our work is original art direction and browser-based interaction built on generously released open-source typefaces. The type designers deserve the credit for the letters. We get to play with what they make possible.
